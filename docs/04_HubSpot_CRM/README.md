# HubSpot CRM & SaaS Operations

## Project Overview

Practical exploration of HubSpot CRM for company record management, deal pipeline tracking and CRM reporting.

## Implementation Evidence

1. Company Records – 32 configured company records.
2. Company Profile – Individual company information and properties.
3. Sales Pipeline – 16 deals across pipeline stages.
4. Reports & Analytics – CRM reporting and dashboard exploration.

## Learning Areas

* CRM configuration and company management.
* Sales pipeline and deal tracking.
* Customer relationship management.
* CRM reporting and analytics.
* SaaS revenue and customer operations concepts.

**Platform:** HubSpot CRM
