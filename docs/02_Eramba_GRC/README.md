# Eramba – Governance, Risk & Compliance Implementation

## 1. Project Overview

This project demonstrates the deployment, configuration and practical implementation of the Eramba Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) platform.

The implementation focuses on establishing a structured risk management environment for a small-scale manufacturing business.

The objective was to apply practical Enterprise Risk Management (ERM), Operational Risk Management (ORM), risk assessment, risk treatment and compliance management concepts using an open-source GRC platform.

## 2. Project Objectives

* Implement a centralised GRC platform.
* Establish structured business risk management.
* Configure and assess business risks.
* Establish risk treatment workflows.
* Support risk and control management.
* Explore compliance management capabilities.
* Validate the implemented platform and workflows.

## 4. Deployment Architecture

The application was deployed using Docker-based services.

Key components:

* Eramba application container
* MySQL database container
* Redis service
* Caddy public proxy
* Cron scheduling service
* Queue processing services

Docker Compose was used to manage the application environment and supporting services.

## 5. Implementation Activities

### 5.1 Platform Deployment

* Prepared the Docker environment.
* Retrieved the required application images.
* Configured application services.
* Deployed the platform using Docker Compose.
* Verified application accessibility.

### 5.2 Business Risk Configuration

Configured the GRC environment around the business risk management requirements of a manufacturing operation.

The implementation focused on operational and business risks rather than limiting the exercise to information security.

### 5.3 Risk Assessment and Treatment

Worked with the platform's risk assessment and treatment capabilities.

Activities included:

* Risk identification.
* Risk assessment.
* Risk prioritisation.
* Treatment strategy selection.
* Mitigation planning.
* Risk management documentation.

### 5.4 Compliance Management

Explored and configured relevant compliance management capabilities within the platform.

### 5.5 Risk and Control Management

Applied risk and control management concepts to support the broader GRC implementation.

## 6. Validation

Functional validation of the implementation was completed.

Validation covered:

* Platform accessibility.
* Application deployment.
* Configured business risk workflows.
* Risk assessment and treatment functionality.
* Supporting application services.

## 7. Key Learning Outcomes

* Practical exposure to an enterprise GRC platform.
* Understanding of GRC platform architecture.
* Docker-based application deployment.
* Business risk configuration.
* Risk assessment and treatment workflows.
* Practical application of operational risk management concepts.
* Understanding of application services and infrastructure dependencies.

## 8. Project Scope and Limitations

This implementation is a practical portfolio project based on a simulated small-scale manufacturing business.

It is not represented as a production deployment or an independently audited enterprise GRC programme.

Power BI and Tableau were implemented as independent analytics projects and were not integrated with Eramba.
