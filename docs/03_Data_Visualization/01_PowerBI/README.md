# Power BI – Enterprise & Operational Risk Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

This project demonstrates the application of Microsoft Power BI for Enterprise Risk Management (ERM), Operational Risk Management (ORM), and SaaS Risk Analytics.

The dashboards provide visual insights into risk exposure, risk classifications, key risk indicators and operational performance.

## 2. Dashboard Components

### Dashboard 1 – Enterprise Risk Dashboard

* Enterprise risk overview.
* Risk categorisation and classification.
* Inherent and residual risk exposure.
* Risk distribution and monitoring.

### Dashboard 2 – Enterprise Risk KPIs

* Key Risk Indicators (KRIs).
* Risk severity monitoring.
* Risk score analysis.
* Operational risk performance.

### Dashboard 3 – SaaS Risk Dashboard

* SaaS operational risk monitoring.
* SaaS risk exposure.
* Risk classification and assessment.
* Monitoring of SaaS-related operational risks.

## 3. Data Sources

The dashboards are based on project datasets developed using:

* Enterprise Risk Register.
* Operational Risk Register.
* SaaS Risk Register.
* Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA).
* SaaS Revenue Management data.

## 4. Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* Power Query
* DAX
* Microsoft Excel
* Risk Analytics
* Data Modelling and Visualisation

## 5. Business Applications

The dashboards demonstrate how data analytics can support:

* Enterprise risk monitoring.
* Operational risk identification.
* Management reporting.
* Risk-based decision-making.
* Key Risk Indicator monitoring.
* SaaS operational risk analysis.

## 6. Project Deliverables

* Three interactive Power BI report pages.
* Enterprise Risk Dashboard.
* Enterprise Risk KPI Dashboard.
* SaaS Risk Dashboard.
* Risk analytics and visual reporting.

## 7. Project Status

Status: Completed

Power BI was implemented as the data analytics and visualisation component of the broader GRC and Operational Risk Management portfolio.
