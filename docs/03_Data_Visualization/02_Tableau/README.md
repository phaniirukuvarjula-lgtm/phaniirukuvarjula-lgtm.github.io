# Tableau – SaaS Risk & Revenue Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

Developed interactive Tableau dashboards to analyse SaaS operational risks, revenue performance and Key Risk Indicators (KRIs).

## 2. Dashboard Components

* SaaS Risk and Revenue Analytics.
* SaaS KRI Monitoring Dashboard.
* Risk metrics and performance visualisation.
* Historical KRI monitoring.

## 3. Data Sources

* SaaS Risk Register.
* SaaS Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA).
* SaaS CRM and Revenue Management datasets.
* KRI monitoring data.

## 4. Tools & Technologies

* Tableau Desktop.
* Microsoft Excel.
* Data Visualisation.
* Risk Analytics.

## 5. Business Applications

* SaaS risk monitoring.
* Revenue performance analysis.
* KRI tracking.
* Risk-based management reporting.

## 6. Project Deliverables

* Interactive SaaS Risk Dashboard.
* SaaS Revenue Analytics.
* KRI Monitoring Worksheets.
* Historical Risk Monitoring.

**Project Status:** Completed.
