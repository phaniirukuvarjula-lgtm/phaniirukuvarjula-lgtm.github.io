Mini RCSA Walkthrough – Manufacturing Operations

An end-to-end Risk and Control Self-Assessment walkthrough based on a practical manufacturing risk scenario.

  1. Risk Scenario: Laser cutting machine breakdown causes production delays.
  2. Inherent Risk: Assess the likelihood and impact before considering controls.
  3. Existing Controls: Preventive maintenance, machine inspections and backup production arrangements.
  4. Residual Risk: Reassess the risk after considering control effectiveness.
  5. Risk Treatment: Strengthen maintenance schedules, escalation procedures and contingency planning.
  6. KRI Monitoring: Track machine downtime, with a project threshold of more than 20 hours per month.

KRI Definitions & Escalation Matrix

This section demonstrates how operational risks in a manufacturing environment can be monitored through Key Risk Indicators (KRIs), defined thresholds, escalation procedures and corrective actions.

Monitoring approach: Project-defined thresholds are used to identify exceptions and support timely management action.

Risk Indicator Green Amber Red Monitoring Escalation / Response
Machine Downtime Below 15 hours/month* 15–20 hours/month* Above 20 hours/month Monthly Escalate to Production Manager; investigate downtime and review preventive maintenance.
Production Delay Rate Below 8%* 8–10%* Above 10% of orders Monthly Review production bottlenecks, scheduling and delivery commitments.
Raw Material Cost Increase Below 7%* 7–10%* Above 10% Monthly Escalate to Procurement; review supplier pricing and sourcing alternatives.
Reporting / Data Errors No recurring errors* Repeated minor errors* Repeated material errors Monthly Investigate root cause, validate source data and strengthen review controls.
Power Continuity Exceptions No material interruption Minor interruption* Any material interruption Monthly / Event-driven Escalate to Operations; review backup power and continuity arrangements.
Regulatory Compliance Exceptions No material exception Potential exception* Any material regulatory exception Monthly / Event-driven Escalate to management; assess compliance impact and corrective action.
Supplier Availability / Dependency Within defined limits* Approaching exception limit* Management-defined exception limit breached Monthly Review supplier dependency, availability and alternate sourcing options.
Customer Concentration Stable customer / sector mix* Notable change in mix* Material change in customer / sector mix Monthly Escalate to management; assess revenue concentration and diversification options.

Note: *Green and Amber bands are illustrative proposed monitoring levels. Red thresholds reflect the project-defined KRI criteria. Management should validate and approve operating thresholds.

KRI Monitoring & Reporting Process

  1. Collect operational and control data from relevant business processes.
  2. Compare actual performance against defined KRI thresholds.
  3. Identify exceptions and assign Green, Amber or Red status.
  4. Escalate significant breaches to the relevant risk or process owner.
  5. Document corrective actions, ownership and target completion dates.
  6. Review trends and update the Risk Register and RCSA where required.

Dashboard Integration: The monitoring approach is designed to support operational risk reporting through Grafana and Tableau dashboards, using KRI status, exception history and trend analysis.
