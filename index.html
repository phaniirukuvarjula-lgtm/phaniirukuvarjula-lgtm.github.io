About Me

I am a commerce graduate with professional experience in business operations and customer-focused processes, developing my expertise in Operational Risk Management, Enterprise Risk Management and Governance, Risk & Compliance.


My practical project work focuses on applying risk management methodologies to a small-scale manufacturing business and SaaS business scenarios. The work includes risk identification, Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA), control evaluation, risk treatment, compliance management and monitoring.


I have worked with platforms and analytical tools including Eramba, CISO Assistant, Power BI, Tableau, Grafana, Excel and HubSpot CRM to develop practical risk management and monitoring capabilities.
