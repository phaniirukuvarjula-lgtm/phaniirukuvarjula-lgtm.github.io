# Operational Risk Management, ERM & GRC Implementation Portfolio

**Practical Enterprise Risk Management (ERM), Operational Risk Management (ORM), Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA), Fraud Risk Analysis, and GRC Platform Implementation.**

## Project Overview

This portfolio demonstrates the practical application of Enterprise Risk Management and Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) concepts within a small-scale manufacturing business.

The project focuses on identifying operational and financial risks, assessing inherent and residual risk exposure, evaluating internal controls, developing mitigation strategies, and establishing risk-monitoring mechanisms.

It also explores how GRC platforms and business intelligence tools can support risk management, reporting, and decision-making.

## Business Context

The project uses a small-scale manufacturing business operating across the following functions:

* Laser Cutting
* Powder Coating
* Surface Finishing
* Molding

The risk model addresses operational disruptions, equipment failures, supplier dependency, raw material cost fluctuations, inventory theft, distributor fraud, production delays, and other business risks.

## Project Objectives

* Develop an Enterprise Risk Management framework.
* Identify and assess operational and fraud risks.
* Implement Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA).
* Evaluate control effectiveness and control gaps.
* Establish risk mitigation and monitoring mechanisms.
* Apply SaaS risk management and revenue analytics concepts.
* Explore practical GRC platform implementation.
* Develop management dashboards for risk visibility and reporting.

## Implementation Approach

The project follows a practical risk management lifecycle:

1. Business process understanding.
2. Risk identification.
3. Inherent risk assessment.
4. Control identification and evaluation.
5. Residual risk assessment.
6. Risk treatment planning.
7. KRI definition and monitoring.
8. Dashboard development and reporting.
9. GRC platform implementation and validation.

## Project Scope & Limitations

This is a practical portfolio and learning implementation based on a representative manufacturing business.

* Power BI and Tableau reporting assets are maintained separately from Eramba.
* No live API integration between the BI tools and Eramba has been implemented.
* Basel III stress-testing functionality has not been implemented as a native platform capability.
* Grafana work represents dashboard exploration rather than a complete production monitoring deployment.
* CISO Assistant represents practical platform exploration, not an enterprise production deployment.
